# Websites to read about AI

With 3 out of 7 posts about artificial intelligence (AI) as of July 27, you might be able to tell that I'm into it. Ever since the days of [MarI/O](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qv6UVOQ0F44), I've been amazed by what machines can do, and this few years only ramped it up like crazy. At the same time, people talked about AI risks, which went from pretty hypothetical to things we only have one attempt at getting right. They're scary. Today, I read about AI to know not only what's capable, but what the future will look like.

Here's a list of websites where I read about AI. They're much more in-depth than what you need to know to use AI, but not as hard to read as research papers.

## The list

Sorted alphabetically.

- [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/ai/): Career advice research with an emphasis on AI due to how impactful it can get. Even if you're not looking for a job, lots of information there can help you understand AI better. Easier to read than other websites in this list.
- [AI Digest](https://theaidigest.org/): Blog posts and interactive demos on AI capabilities.
  - [AI Village](https://theaidigest.org/village): Watch 20+ AI agents use a computer to accomplish tasks, interact in a group chat, and goof around.
- [AI Futures Project](https://www.aifutures.org/): A research group forecasting the future of AI using simulations, models, tabletop exercises, etc. Their predictions are regarded as pessimistic by some, but some of the authors have a strong track record in forecasting.
  - [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/): A forecast made in 2025, in the form of a scenario, that AI will automate AI research and development in 2027, causing recursive improvement.
  - [AI 2040](https://ai-2040.com/): A policy recommendation for the United States, in the form of a scenario, on ensuring that AI development goes well and humans flourish.
  - [AI Futures Model](https://www.aifuturesmodel.com/): A mathematical model predicting the growth of AI capabilities. Research-level reading difficulty, and requires plenty of prior knowledge.
- [Andon Labs](https://andonlabs.com/): Researching the ability for AI agents to run organisations. They are letting AI agents run radio stations, a shop, and a cafe as experiments.
- [ARC Prize](https://arcprize.org/): Researching the ability for AI models to adapt to new problems.
  - [ARC-AGI-3](https://arcprize.org/arc-agi/3): Their latest benchmark, where AI agents attempt to solve many instruction-less puzzle games. Try playing the games yourself.
- [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/): Data gatherer and analyst of AI development trends.
- [Forethought](https://www.forethought.org/): Think tank on how to prepare for transformative AI.
- [METR](https://metr.org/): Evaluating autonomous and concerning capabilities of AI, famously through "time horizons", the average size of programming tasks AI can complete consistently.
- [Neuronpedia](https://www.neuronpedia.org/): Interactive demos of interpretability research. This is the field studying how AI thinks, much like psychology studies how humans think.
