## About me

Hi! I'm Ming Hin Shi (施銘軒) — Ming Hin (銘軒) is my first name, and Shi (施) is my surname.

- I'm a University of Hong Kong graduate with a major in computer science and a minor in psychology.
- I started wanting to make video games — not AAA games, but indie games like those you can find on [itch.io](https://itch.io) or play on Adobe Flash.
- Later, I was fascinated by psychology research, especially Daniel Kahneman's [_Thinking, Fast and Slow_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow) and related behavioural economics.
- Today, I'm encouraged by [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/) to pivot towards [AI safety research](https://80000hours.org/career-reviews/ai-safety-technical-research/). But first, I'm looking for a machine learning research assistant/engineer position to hone my skills and test my fit.
